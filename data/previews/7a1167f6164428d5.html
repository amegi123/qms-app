  • PURPOSE

The purpose of this procedure is to define the process of Annual& semi-annual Physical Inventory in WF. An inventory can be taken at any time using this process; however a full physical inventory must be taken in December 8& July7to have a reconciled inventory for the fiscal year and to provide data for the determination of cost of service, cost of annual ending inventory & inventory report.


2.  SCOPE

The scope of   this procedure applies to all materials annual inventory in WFfrom the counting period up to the final report period. It takes approximately from 45-60 days from starting date usually December 8& July7of each year.

1.

Notify the warehouse and all department

All annual inventories must be scheduled. This annual inventory schedule informs all user departments by official letter before 3 week from starting date. All responsibility ofWFemployee to accomplish the annual inventory period effectively, it needs more coordination and time management.

The goal of the annual inventory count is to obtained accurate information about inventories on hand which should be help to make right business decision, an alternative to a full physical inventory when all inventory is counted at a point in time is inventory cycle counts when you count inventory in portion throughout a year.

 Important reasons why a company conduct a physical inventory?

  • To verify or ascertain the physical count, condition and location of merchandise & non- merchandise inventory items and to ensure that it is properly documented.
  • To identify, document and add items to its inventory list that are on-hand and meet qualifying criteria but are not currently shown as part of inventory.
  • To ensure that legitimately transferred or disposed of items are no longer carried on the inventory listing and finally to identify any missing or damaged items that need to be located, repaired or replaced.
  • The most obvious reasons are that you conduct a physical inventory to check it the inventory accounting record are accurate and complete at a particular time.
  • Physical inventory is not only an accounting requirement but also accurate stock quantity of WFmore likely to be successful completion of constructed house and also have better control of company stock levels and company money.
  • Should be to order the goods in the right quantity at the proper time avoiding over or under stock.

2.

Organization Of a committee member 

A committee composed of three- sevenmembers to be organized at head office and branches level also designated by General Manager/ Deputy General Manager then orientation needs to be given based on this procedure on how to undertake inventory. The committees’ member needs to have the ability and experience of undertaking inventory and reporting on their finding and observation.

More than one committee can be organized for fixed assets, Non-fixed assets, stock items and vehicles inventory to minimize work load.

A committee which designated for the head office and branches might be segregated its duties by types of assets or locations to minimize work load.

3

Planning & Preparation 

A physical inventory is a time and resource consuming procedure which requires proper planning. The planning helps the warehouse and the committee member to make annual physical inventory counts more effective.

Establishment and documentation of counting procedure will allow to control and supervise physical inventory properly.

First, should be determining a date and time of conducting a physical inventory and inform all departments about it. Physical inventory counting takes place on different days because of several remote warehouse select a day when the company activities low.

Second, choose the methods of conducting a physical inventory in WFselect the best method, this method is A COUNT SHEET METHOD this method pre-printed lists of inventory (usually generated by company soft copy) are used to record on hand count.

This method the easiest and cheapest method less time consuming, searching counting, comparing and recording. A lot of time spent to find particular items during wall to wall counts items which are not in the listing or not in their place could be missed.

Third, should be prepare the warehouse make sure that all stock items are in their proper places and can be clearly identified clean up the store area, label all shelves and location, slow moving and nonmoving items can be counted and marked a day before a full physical inventory. Identify damage, discounted and absolute classes of items places them separately from inventory prepare and provide the plan of stock location. 

In the last make sure all inventory is clearly identified and located in its assigned location the warehouse multiple location for items consolidation in to as few location as possible by combining smaller. 

Don’t move, send or receive any material while your count is in progress containing it twice or missing it completely.

Executing a physical Inventory

A physical inventory process must be an independent position this means cannot be responsible for check in and receiving persons the committee member  organized out of this responsibility persons it reduce /avoid the internal theft.

Counting committee should not be given access to the inventory quantities recorded in the accounting record. This is called a blind count and helps to eliminate guessing on the part of counters as they would not know what the quantity is.

Stop productionoperation (inventory transaction) on the day of counting or exercise proper control over movement of inventories. Do not make any movement of items either in to or out of the warehouse (even if they are misplaced). Do not fill orders or receives good during the count period there is a great risk of missing any items or there double count.

  • Proper control over count cards (tags) sheet is needed ensure that all cards sheets issued to counting member are sequentially numbered accounted for and returned after the counting process is finished including those which were voided or unused.
  • Immediately compare your counts with accounting records audit the counts and conducts recount if there are unacceptable differences.
  • Mark counted items and location. This should be to ensure inventory completeness i.e.no missing items or items counted twice which an important aspect of any physical inventory is.
  • When verifying your counts. Give special attention to fast moving and volume items such items usually have a greater risk of counting errors in comparing to slow moving items.

Responsible committee member verifies that the counts in the warehouses are accurate the quantities can be entered in your computer. Find several counting errors in an area the entire section should be recounted. When verification is successfully completed all counts are entered in to the computer.

Disposal of Surplus, Scrap and Waste


The final objective of inventory control is the detection of inactive stock items that may add to inventory investment without contributing any corresponding value.  Inactive items may arise due to several factors  such as:- 


  • Over acquisition, over buying and over delivering 
  • Change in design and specification
  • Abandonment of project or cancellation of contracts 
  • Undetected errors in materials accounting
  • Error in record keeping or breakage  

Surpluses, scrap and waste have to be dispose. Disposal returns to avoiding either through discarding or selling the items with book value. 

Proper disposal of materials should be undertaken in conformance with the company policy and the dissection of committee member. 


The following alternative possibilities can be used for disposal of in active items. 

  • Utilization /Cancelled from stock balance
  • Utilization by transfer to another project
  • Utilization by charge out/ discount on standard costs 
  • Return to original supplier
  • Sales at book value on surplus scrap or salvage materials.
