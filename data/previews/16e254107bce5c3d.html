5.2 Process Steps Description

FLW

Process Description

1

Production Order Registrations 

  • The approved production order sent from ODH, will be registered by PH.

2

Planning 

  • The planning and scheduling of Man, Machine, Material and Method shall be done by PH, TH&the implementation is evaluated by ODH.

3

Raw Material Requisition & Receiving 

  • PH&/orSUP, SLshall be responsible to request and receive the necessary raw material after approved by ODH.

4-6

Raw Material Preparation, Mixing And Loading

  • Labor workers are responsibleforpreparing, mixing and loading raw material as desired and supervisors & Shift Leaders are responsible for coordinating and checking the overall activities performed during raw material preparation, mixing and loading process.

7

Machine Setup

  • OPR & La are responsible for conducting all necessary actions to ready machine for production and PH, SUP are responsible for assisting, coordinating and checking the machine conditioning process.

8

Production (Extrusion/ Injection Molding)

  • SUP,SL, OPR&Laare responsible for monitoring all necessary actions during the production process: Clamping, Plasticizing, Injection& Extrusion, die forming and Cooling processes and SUPare responsible in assisting and coordinating the above activities.

9-10

QA Inspection and Conforming & Non-Conforming  Product Identification

  • The QA, PH, SUPand SL are responsible toidentifyconforming and non-conforming products.

11-13

Printing and Transportation

  • If conforming product needs printing, it will be performed by OPR,La, SUPand SL control overall activities performed during printing and transportation of final plastic products to store.
  • If conforming product does not need printing then the product directly goes to finished product store by OPR &La.

14

Finished Product Transfer

  • SUP &Production clerk is responsible to transfer the conformed products to store







6. RISKS

  • Human error in registering production order (quantity and types)
  • improper planning (to wrongly matched machine and product order)
  • Repetitive batch processing leading to pillage of Non –conforming products and plastic purges leading to increased crushing and low out put
  • Work place hazard due to leakages from hydraulic fluid, water and injuries occurring through faulty operations 
  • Lack of specialized skill in setting up operation parameters leading to high rates of scraps  
  • Delay to meet work order due to mechanical problem
  • Too much time taken in punch – cavity and mold mounting procedures
  • Human error in weighing and proportionate raw materials leading to non-conformity in product 
  • Improper cutter & sizing adjustment leading to size length variation and frequent stops (operation interruption)
  • Risk in manual counting of products
