5.2 Description of process steps

FLW

Description 

1

PS, Purchaser shall receive the Approved Purchase Request and consult with the user Department Head / PSD to proceed with the purchasing process.

2

PSD Head or the General Manager make theDecision on the purchasing of  Local or foreign market.

3

Verification of Authority and Budget 

The responsibility of the Materials Management Section or user department is to give details of items with full descriptions.  However, the responsibility of the PSD is to check whether the PR is within the budget limit or not and also checking the accuracy of the document.  Budget reference number writes in the purchase requisition.

4,5

Request for Quotation or Bids /Price Quotation/ 

This refers to identification and analysis of possible sources of supply.  A Quotation is a statement (Performa) acquired from potential suppliers.  Requests for Quotation are a process of initiating potential suppliers that are willing to compete to supply the required material.  

There are two types of Bids:

Open Bid– It is a type of bid where all potential suppliers are invited through mass media and advertising agents to participate in the bidding.

Closed Bid– In this case selected suppliers are approached (invited) to participate in the bid.  Here, the invitation can be made through personal contact, telephone or formal letter.

Issuing of Enquires and Tender Collection 

In the case of closed bid the company use letters or printed enquiry form because it knows the bidder.  But in the case of Open tender, the company use mass media.

Preparation of the tender involves:

  • Bidding Notice
  • Bidder’s instruction
  • Description of Materials
  • Samples and blue prints (when required)
  • Tender Documents

In order to give general description of the materials; delivery time and point, term of payment, last date of submitting bids, general term and condition should be included.  

In the process of collecting tenders the company need to set up separate boxes & seal the boxes on the due date & time.  After collecting the tender, the company needs to open the tender.  Tender opening involves the following activities.

  • Earnest money deposit
  • Time, date & place should be specified 
  • If the service or material needs special expertise or professionalism, virtual/technical team will be assigned.
  • In normal bidder selection when the money is below 100,000.00 the purchasing committee will open the tender.

These and other things should enter the tender opening register or minute. The minute should contain price, delivery date, discount, delivery point, & others if any.

Here suppliers that do not pay bid bonds should get back their letters unopened and bid letters arriving between the closing date and the opening date should also be returned to the supplier unopened.

  • Note: on some cases potential suppliers will be contacted directly and the purchasing will be executed with them.

6

Opening Of Performa Invoice

The request depends on the type of materials because some materials need price quotations and other not in the company items that have less than 5,000 birr value purchase directly without quotation; and 5001-50,000birr need Performa invoice. Above 100,001 birr the notice should be advertised on mass media as newspapers ,collected Performa opens by purchase committee member all purchases opened by  purchase committee opened Performa approved by General Manager or assigned person.

7

Sample Approval

The requested material specifications Prepared and material quality approved by user department by using sample or by visiting supplier’s premises.

8,9

Evaluation of Bids & Selection of Suppliers

This is analysis of the potential suppler who fulfill the criteria of the company.  Suppliers are evaluated /reviewed by the following factors or selection criteria.

        ► In terms of price proposed by the suppliers.

        ► In terms of discount they offer.

        ► In terms of the shipping terms

        ► In terms of delivery date

        ► In terms of reliability of the supplier by analyzing past performance

        ► In terms of reciprocity (this refers to mutual interchange of favors, privileges in good sense)

        ► In terms of quality of work & other services

Here the lowest bidder usually wins the tender, but sometimes this bidder may be denied or not awarded to supply. This happens because of the following reasons;

  • A higher bidder may provide better after sale service.
  • A higher bidder may have better plant facilities and be able to offer quality provided than the low bidder.
  • The low bidder may not be reliable.
  • The reciprocity may favor some company other than the low bidder.
  • The low bidder may be located so far from the company.
  • Exceptional goodwill may exist between higher bidder & the buyer.
  • The company may adhere to the policy of dealing with local suppliers. 

Local Purchase Evaluation Sheet

  • After the opening of Performa invoice purchase evaluation sheet prepared by PS/Purchaser.
  • Purchase evaluation sheet is verified by PSD and finally approved by GM.

10,11

Issuing of Purchase Order

The Purchase Requisition gives the purchaser to order the materials described in the requisitions.  The Procurement Section / Purchaser should maintain or have access to an up-to-date list of vendors, which includes price, available discounts, estimated delivery time, and any other relevant information.  From this list, the Purchasing Section / Purchaser selects a vender from whom high quality materials can be obtained when needed at competitive cost is selected.  Then the purchaser completes a Purchase Order, which is a purchasing document that formalizes a purchase transaction with the seller.  So a Purchase Order is a serially numbered document that is used to make purchases.

The Purchase Order should contain the following information. 

  • Suppliers name and address. 
  • Quotation reference and date.
  • Description of items.
  • Quantity Ordered.
  • Unit Price.
  • Terms of Payment.
  • Date of the order.
  • Delivery date and 
  • Signature of the purchasing agent

Issue Payment Request

After approval of evaluation sheet & signed contract agreements to request the payment from finance department.

                          Payment Request

  • Payment request requested by purchase officer.
  • Payment request finally approved by GM.

12

Follow-up and expediting the Order

Follow-up refers to reminding the supplier to insure the timely arrival of the Order. And expediting is speeding up or accelerating the receipt of the item before the agreed-up on time.  The later requires good relationship between the company & supplier.

In WF, we will use manually & computerized systems. Manually system is a calendar follow up system. This is keeping the follow up order on the required date when the next follow up is scheduled or suppliers list.This is filling order alphabetically on the top we are going to use a number running from 1 to 30/31 and put a visible colored mark on the date the next follow up is made. 

13

Receiving, Inspecting and Storing 

When the items are received, the following receiving procedures are usually adopted.

  • Unpacking and checking the material – Compare for the right material that the packing ship contains with the materials received.  Here, we need to check for the right quantity and General Condition of the material.
  • Completing the receiving report and distribute this report to:
  • Delivery of materials: these materials may be delivered to the user department or to the Store.  Finally, whoever is received the material will sign on the receiving report.

Inspection Report:

Whenever it is necessary to take technical inspection, we may make sample/all inspection.  This depends on the nature of material and/or on the description of those materials.  Based on this technical report, if the material is not useful (partly or wholly) the purchasing people will notify the supplier how to handle those items.  In this case the possible courses of action would be:

1.  Return the material to the supplier.

2.  Keep some of the more acceptable material and return the rest.

3.  Keep all and rework it to the point where it is acceptable.

When is Inspection Justified?

This depends on economies of scale, which is based on two basic costs.

i.  Cost of Inspection

ii. Costs resulting from defectiveness entering the production operation and/or reaching customers.

14

Closing the Order

After making the necessary payment to the vendor, closing an order simply entails a consolidation of all documents and correspondence relevant to the order in filling them in closed order file which include a purchase requisition, the open order file of the purchase order, the acknowledgement, the receiving report, the inspection report and any note or correspondence pertaining to the order.

5.3. Supplementary Issues

5.3.1. Important Issues in Purchasing

i. Make or Buy decision

ii. Organization of Purchasing 

a. Centralized Purchasing

This occurs when the authority and responsibility to handle the material needs of the company is given to one department.

b. Decentralized Purchasing 

This is to apply the project purchaser to purchase the material items of low value and of routine use are purchased under decentralization.

C. Computerized Purchasing 

This occurs when both the centralized and decentralized purchasing are operated in a company.  Items of high value and those that require high technical analysis and knowledge of experts are purchased under centralized purchasing.  


5.3.2 Type of Purchasing 

This is the right time aspect of purchasing.  The quality of materials affects the price and time of purchase.  The company should have time of purchase for their construction process, because materials could have increase/decrease in price seasonably depending on the circumstances.  

Here based on time dimension, there are four time-based types of purchasing.  These are 

  • Hand to mouth
  • Current Requirement
  • Forward buying 
  • Speculative buying

5.3.3. Discounts in Purchasing 

The other important factor which can influence the Procurement Section / Purchaser insuppler selection or tender evaluation is discount.  A discount is a reduction allowed by a seller to a purchasing department by the following discount methods

i. Cash Discount

ii. Quantity Discounts

iii. Trade Discounts

6. RISKS

  • Unclear/Incorrect purchasing information
  • Reviews and Approvals take too long
  • Collecting Proforma take too long
  • Lack of updated supplier list
  • Biased evaluation
  • Lack of required material in the market
  • Miscounting of materials
  • Typing error
  • Ambiguity of contracts
  • Sellers may not supply as per the prior contractual agreement & specifications or may supply low quality products.  
  • Goods in-transit may be lost, destroyed by fire, damaged due to different reasons. 
  • The supplier may deliver products that reach its expiry date
  • Delay of payments 
  • Overlooking insignificant non-conformities of purchased materials when inspecting
  • Delay of delivery
