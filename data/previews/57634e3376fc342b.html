4.1 Definition


For the purpose of this procedure the definitions given on ISO 9000:2015 and relevant product specifications apply.


Production: the action of making or manufacturing from components or raw materials.


Production Plan: The process of planning a production by utilizing the resource allocation of activities of employees, materials and production capacity, in order to serve different customers.

    • Process Step Description 


FLW

Description

1

Marketing and Sales Department will send the work order to ODH / PPafter participating a bid or receiving customer order.

2

The ODH / PP will review the WO in consultation with other sections considering:

  • The availability of man power, 
  • Machines capacity,
  • Raw material availability,
  • Accessories,
  • The nature of the requirement. 

After reviewing the order ODH / PP will accept the order or will reject (the rejection is mostly regarding delivery time). 

3

Since delivery date is one of the main criteria for customers satisfaction, according to the capacity and daily target the production planner fix delivery date of product, and the ODH / PP will inform sales section to communicate with clients to proceed to production planning.

4

The Production Planner prepares Production plan based on the issued work order and forward it for approval toODH, GM.  

5&6

If the production plan approved, the PP will prepare production order based on his plan which includes customer name, production order no., order date, delivery date, style, colour, raw materials and other consumables approved by ODH. If it is not approved go back to step 4.

The approved Production Order shall be sent to the PH and the QA.

7&8

PP will control the status of the production process by checking the starting status (Type & date), Daily Production Status, Delivery status.

After checking all the above mentioned status of the production PP will report to ODH and accordingly ODHwill take action based on the status report.













  • RISKS
  • Customer’s requirements not registered properly on the WO
  • Delay of WO
  • Lack of updated information on the Production Status, Material Availability, Man Power Availability & Machine Status 
  • Argument with customers on delivery time take too long
  • Computers & Software breakdowns
  • Approval of plan take too long
  • Missing information during preparation on PO
  • Accidents during controlling the production status
  • Report preparation takes too long
