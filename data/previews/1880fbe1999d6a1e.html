INTRODUCTION

Water Fit General Trading is committed to protect and safeguard its people and stakeholders by creating safe & healthy working environment.

In line with the WHO & Ethiopian Ministry of Health Guiding Principles on Covid-19 Pandemic prevention & Response, WF recognizes the corporate responsibility to comply and commit to prevent the life of its people. 

POLICYOBJECTIVES

The objective of this Water Fit’s Health & Safety Policy is to provide a direction & a guideline for its employees and business partners about the COVID-19 Pandemic Prevention & Response. 

POLICY SCOPE

WF’s Health & Safety Policy applies to all employees, anyone doing business for or with WF and others acting on WF’s behalf. This applies to all locations where WF conducts business and to all company-sponsored events.

DEFINITIONS

COVID-19 Pandemic: an infectious disease caused by severe acute respiratory syndrome corona virus 2 (SARS-CoV-2). It was first identified in December 2019 in Wuhan, Hubei, China, and has resulted in an ongoing pandemic. 

Health & Safety: an organization's statement regarding its commitment to maintaining a safe and healthy work environment.

GUIDELINES

Based on the international & national Health & Safety guidelines on Covid-19 prevention & Response, the following guidelines shall be applied in WF: 

Prevention precautions at work places 

Employees are obligated to follow the general hygiene rules, such as: 

Wash your hands after using the toilet, before eating, and if you cough/sneeze into your hands (follow the 20-second hand-washing rule). You can also use the sanitizers you’ll find around the office.

Cough/sneeze into your sleeve, preferably into your elbow. If you use a tissue, discard it properly and clean/sanitize your hands immediately.

Open the windows regularly to ensure open ventilation.

Avoid touching your face, particularly eyes, nose, and mouth with your hands to prevent from getting infected.

If you find yourself coughing/sneezing on a regular basis, avoid close physical contact with your coworkers and take extra precautionary measures (such as requesting sick leave).

Clean your machineries and tools after using.

Screening 

All employees’ and visitors’ Temperature shall be screened at the gate. If the temperature is above the normal state, it should be reported to the office and the employee &/or the visitor shall not be entered the premises

Using Personal Protective Equipments (PPE), Disinfectants &Cleaning Responsibilities

All employees should wear face masks when entered the premises and during works.

Keep your masks and other protection materials clean at all times

By using the available disinfectant clean your work station.

Use only your PPE for yourself and always keep it clean.

Infection control responses

If any employee has cold symptoms, such as cough/sneezing/fever, or feels poorly, request sick leave or work from home.

If any employee has a positive COVID-19 diagnosis, they can return to the office only after they’ve fully recovered, with a doctor’s note confirming your recovery.

Work sites & Work hour arrangements

 By following the government’s regulations and directives, working hours shall be arranged to avoid and minimize social contacting and movements in the city. The arrangements shall be communicated to all employees and stakeholders accordingly.


Travel Restrictions

All work trips and events – both domestic and international – will be cancelled/postponed until further notice.

In-person meetings should be done with a careful distancing and ventilated room.

If you normally commute to the office by public transportation and do not have other alternatives, it should be done with a careful distancing and prevention.

If you are planning to travel voluntarily to a high-risk city with increased COVID-19 cases, we’ll ask you to work from home for 14 calendar days. You will also be asked not to come into physical contact with any colleagues during this time.

Risk identification &mitigation resources 

WF shall always follow the data released by the Ministry of Health to identify risks per zones and plan the mitigation accordingly.

System for tracking cases & consultation for emergency response

The first-line emergency response contacts for consultation and tracking cases shall be available.


Healthcare services

WF will follow its employees’ healthcare protocol to handle healthcare during this pandemic. 

Communication means & levels (Internal & External)

All employees shall communicate their concerns, information and questions to their immediate supervisors or to the respective department heads.

Communicating external stakeholders shall be handled by respective departments and the general manager.

Information sources 

The information source shall always be from the government authorities’ regulations & directives and WHO directives. 

Awareness/induction to employees the policy and response plan

This policy shall be communicated to all employees in an effective manner.


RESPONSIBILITY

The Health & Safety policy is owned and maintained by all departments and functions in WF. Top Management is responsible for the creation, administration, updating and communication of the policy.


0COMPLIANCE

Employees and all stakeholders are expected to comply with this and all applicable WF policies. Violation of this policy or the refusal to cooperate will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination and referral to the appropriate authorities.
