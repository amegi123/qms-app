4. DEFINITIONS

4.1DEFINITION

Materials: are basic substances in its natural, modified, or semi processed state used as an input or to operations for subsequent modification or transformation into an output. 


Materials Management (MM):is the process of management which coordinates, supervises and executes the tasks associated with the flow of materials to, through, and out of an organization in an integrated fashion.


Storehouse: a place where items are housed or a building designed for storage purposes and constructed with roof and complete side and end walls.


Store management: it is a process of planning, coordination and control of various activities pertaining to efficient, effective and economic storage and store keeping.


Inspection: to check all materials received.


Storage: to keep everything in its proper place


Issue: to issue materials and goods only when properly authorized.


Store record: to maintain a correct record of any thing received or issued form store.

Stock control: to arrange continuously receipts and issues to ensure that stock balances are sufficient, with due regard to economy.


Stock verification: to physically verify the quantity and condition of goods lying in the store.


Identification: to systematically define and describe all items in stock


Stock accountant: the person recording stock movements and balances in value in computer and stock cards.

Material handling means providing the right amount of the right material, in the right condition, at the right place, in the right position, in the right sequence, and for the right cost, by the right method(s).


Receipt: receipt is the process of accepting from all sources, all materials, equipment and parts used in the company.


Holding: although holding inventory results in additional cost it is necessary to maintain inventory to avoid running out of stock.

2

Identification

The received goods should be identified by a clear way ofidentification. Without identification it is impossible to uniquely identify the materials needed. This problem can be solved using codification. The codification system applied both alphabetical & numerical systems.

All material should be given a code identifying the material specifically, definitely, and unambiguously. Adopting a well-designed codification system enables the MMS/SK to have a unique, precise (i.e. exact), simple and consistent method of identifying stocks. Code should be compact, concise, and flexible enough to accommodate future addition.


Coding of materials appropriately recorded  in the user department and store to give the following advantage:

  • A large number of store items are reduced to smaller group of items in a systematic way.
  • It is easier for storing, recording and accounting to deal with categories or classes of materials rather than individual items.
  • Classification and codification helps easy location of stores.
  • Easy identification of store items facilitates prompt service.
  • Possibility of duplicate stock of the same item kept under a different name or description is avoided.
  • Long descriptions of materials are avoidance. Nomenclature of materials is simplified by choosing symbols& numbers which are simple, clear and free from duplication.
  • Systematic grouping helps in standardization of materials and encourages diverse uses of the same item.
  • It is useful for proper location of stores. It also forms a basis for setting up of different stores.

The material items shall classified by:

  • Develop a unique identification method and describe 

A part from the above codification systems, a system by which the physical location of the items in the store should be identified. Each inventory items should be assigned as specific storage location. 

3

Store Recording

A good warehouse layout and good storage methods yield the following benefits

  • Ready accessibility of major materials.
  • Efficient space utilization and flexibility of arrangement.
  • A reduced need for materials handling equipment 
  • Minimization of materials deterioration & pilferages
  • Ease of physical counting 

The first and foremost attention that should be given while storing of materials is to understand different information’s about the items to be stocked. 

All receipts, issues & transactions must continually and faithfully be recorded/ in bin cards. The balance shall be recorded immediately with the identification number, location and so on.

    • The company  shallapply the following basic Principles of storage:
  • All the materials must be identified and coded. This helps in establishing their nature or identity.
  • Name, class and reference number should be recorded by each item of material.
  • To indicate the materials stored in bins, racks, shelves, etc. it must have signs or tags attached on them.
  • The storeroom should be divided into sections, and the materials/goods belonging to one class should be stored in each section.
  • In order to protect the materials from damage in storage, provision of proper storage space particular must be provided.
  • Floor plan showing the various section of storeroom should be posted indifferent parts of the storeroom. This helps eliminating wastage of time in the location of a particular material.
  • There must be a constant check on the balance on hand of quantities stored. Regular program of inspection, physical verification and maintenance of stores shall be in place.
  • Entry inside stores should be restricted.
  • No materials should be allowed to pass out from the stores, unless a written request of someone in authority is received.
  • Special attention must be given to perishable materials. Inflammable materials such as petrol, kerosene, oil, etc. should be stored in a place far away from the others items.
  • Oldest material should issue first.

4

Store Request

Store Requisition (SR) Shall be Authorized by the User Department Head. Authorized SR shall be reviewed by the Store keeper to check and identify the stock level and stock balance .If the stock is available it shall be recorded in the Store Issue Voucherand dispatched fromstore. If the material is not available or not sufficient,Purchase Requisition (PR) will be prepared. No materials should be allowed to pass out from the stores, unless approved MaterialRequest is received. 

The purchase request must be authorized by the appropriate authority. The authority should see that the material requested is within the budget allocated tothe concerned department.   

Origination of Purchase Requisition (PR)

Purchase Requisition is a document generated by the store that authorizes material purchase.The need for purchase originates in the MMS or in the User department.  This need for purchase is transmitted to the ProcurementSection by approved Purchase Requisition.  The Purchase Requisition is a serially numbered internal document by which the need for purchase is transmitted or communicated.

6

Selection of Fixed Asset 

Fixed Asset consists of all property owned by the company with a useful life of more than one year and a minimum cost of Br 1000 are categorized under this group. Items under 1000 Br are expensed automaticallyand registered by store section.

*Items in the store that has fixed asset nature and not issued from the store until issued date should not be included in this category; rather they will be included as stock items.

After the materials had been delivered, User Department shall be responsible for materials stored in their areas. The material delivered to users shall be used within a month, and it shall not be subjected to a high rate of deterioration or obsolescence Un used Materials shall be returned to store. To determine the actual usage of material during a given period, it is necessary to take a physical count of the material at the end of the period and to compare this figure with the similar beginning-period figures (which has been adjusted for materials receipts during the period). Hence, accounting changes are determined indirectly rather than directly. As a result, the system provides control over material usage only if it is used in conjunction with an accounting system employing standard cost techniques.
