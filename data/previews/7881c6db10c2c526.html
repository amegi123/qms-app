4. DEFINITIONS& ABBREVIATION


4.1 Definitions


Fixed Asset/ Non-current asset: Any tangible or intangible asset that can be used in an operation of WFfor more than a year, and whose purchase price is more than Birr 1000.00is considered as non-current assets. The expected life or operational efficiency of such an asset can be extended or improved by major repair and maintenance works.


Fixed asset includes office building, construction in progress, warehouses, plant and machinery, furniture & equipment, computers, vehicles, leasehold land, land improvements, non-current assets in transit, long term investment, deferred costs, goodwill, etc….


All acquisition of, betterments and improvements on, and disposals of non- current assets are made in accordance with the approved budget, and the authorization of the General Manager.

5.2. Process Step Description

FLW

Description


Identify of Fixed Asset 

Fixed Asset consists of all property owned by the company with a useful life of more than one year and a minimum cost of Br 1000 are categorized under this group. Items under 1000 Br are expensed automatically   and registered by stores and by central procurement &Supply department. This type of fixed asset counts every annual inventory year from the last year inventory compare to the actual year inventory the shortage items report immediately.

*Items in the store that has fixed asset nature and not issued from the store until issued date should not be included in this category; rather they will be included as stock items.

Fixed Asset Register

Fixed Asset register is the format to use the WFfixed asset items separately recorded on registration book or fixed asset cards, this card or registration book contains description, identification number, serial number building size(for building),model, unit of measurement, date of purchase, supplier, date of manufacturer, cost, annual depreciation rate, estimated life, location & table calculate depreciation. The format used to recorded full information regarding the Fixed Asset and to compute depreciation this format to should be kept in the general inventories controller and shall be checked during the inventory year and to control the property condition.

Categories of fixed Assets 


We classify our fixed assets/ non-current assets above1000 Br in 9groups.

The principal accounting Policies adopted by WF are stated below:-

  • FA is stated at cost less accumulated depreciation.
  • Depreciation is computed in accordance with proclamation No.979/2008 on straight line basis using the following per annum
  • FA acquired before Sene 30, 2008E.C would continue to depreciate on a reducing balance method except for buildings which had been depreciated on a straight line basis.
  • LEASE HOLD LAND:-It is stated atcost less amortization. Amortization is charged over a period of lease term.

Recording 

All fixed Asset above 1000 Br should be identified and recorded in a Fixed Asset Register. They will be physically verified in regular intervals and at the time of acquisition each asset should be given an individual identification number.


Identification Number (fixed Asset label) 

The identification number tag to the fixed asset to made for aluminum label or another material.

The existence of identification number tags should be checked on the assets during the inventory and controlling time. If missed then it should be retagged. 

               Please follow the figure on the label of fixed asset  


    e.g.          WF-01-001-001

WF: 

                    Number next to  stands for notifying it is a fixed asset

                    Number in the middle stands for group (asset type)

             The last numbers is for fixed assets serial number

N.B:  each fixed asset’s serial number has to indicate all the information about specific item.

Cost and depreciation 

The cost of the assets should be capitalized and presented to the company balance sheet. Then in each accounting period a depreciation charge is levied on the income and expenditure account to write off the cost of asset as the same time the contra asset account of the asset i.e. accumulated depreciation shown on the Balance Sheet.


Thus we split the cost of these assets out over a number of years to reflect the contribution they make to the service we provide in future years. 

As an alternative to the actual cost competent valuation may be done as a basis for the initial cost for the first year depreciation is calculated from the assets purchased date to end of the year.


Land                                        No Depreciation 

For Building                                             20years                   5%

Computer & Printers  5years                  20%

Others                                                        6.67 years             15%


For fully depreciated fixed assets, their value should be recorded as birr 1.00 to show the asset depreciated fully.

Disposal Of Fixed Asset 


After annual inventory of fixed Asset, the fixed asset items condition properly records by the attached format of the condition of FA describes by very good, good, fair, non –function, damage, by use of this description, the count committee identifies the fixed assets whether non-functional or need maintenance. Non-functional items need maintenance report to general service head to give maintenance & non-functional & damage items separately recorded and return to stores after that the material ready to disposal by the disposal procedure of FA.


Organizing Committees 

A committee composed of three/fiveneedsto be organized for disposal of fixed asset, inventory of fixed asset and any other description to solve by committee.  

A committee member needs to have the ability and experience of undertaking inventory and reporting on their finding and observations.

For Entries

*copy of the invoice or alternatively valuation of the asset by competent evaluator.

*Non-Merchandise Receiving Voucher

During Usage

*Copies of major repair invoices and the respective work order.

For Disposal 

*Police Statement (in case of theft)

*Transfer letter and Non-Merchandise Issue voucher (in case of transfer)

*Committee statement (in case of scrap/)

Asset disposal write-offs and transfers to be authorized by committee member and approve to managing directors.  

Report 

FA annual inventory report recorded all status of the material condition, location, value and necessary document fully record by its categories the completed report submits to Finance Cost Management Department using the attached format.
